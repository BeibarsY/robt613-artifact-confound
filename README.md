# Artifacts as confounds in motor-imagery decoding

ROBT613 Brain–Computer Interfaces, midterm project.
**Team:** Chidiadi Bethel Mba, Sungkar Bolat, Beibars Ybraiakhyn, Ramazan Zypayev.

Do eye movements, rather than sensorimotor rhythms, drive motor-imagery decoding on BCI Competition IV dataset 2a (BNCI2014-001)? The arrow cue points toward the task, and the eyes follow it.

**Result.** The three EOG channels alone decode left vs right hand (H1 supported). Removing ocular activity from the EEG does not lower band-power EEG decoding (H2 and H3 not supported). Details and every decision: `NOTES.md`.
